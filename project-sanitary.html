Educating Sanitary Workers

Occupational health and safety education for sanitary workers.

Public health project

This project focuses on educating sanitary workers about occupational hazards, safety measures, and health guidelines to protect themselves from infections while performing their essential duties.

Occupational SafetyFocus on safe working conditions
Targeted EducationCustomized training for sanitary workers
Health ProtectionPreventative measures against infections
Offline decision rulesWorks for families with a basic smartphone
Chakwal DistrictPiloted with families at home

Key findings

Health GuidelinesClear safety protocols established
AwarenessIncreased knowledge of occupational risks
PreventionMethods to avoid work-related infections
2 physiciansReviewed the rules at DHQ Hospital Chakwal before the pilot
4 illnesses + danger signsMeasles, chickenpox, dengue and malaria, with emergency referral
