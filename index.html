Clinical & Community Pharmacist (Pharm.D)

Dr. Anila Munir

As a pharmacist, I seek to harness my pharmaceutical expertise to address pressing public health challenges through evidence-based and population-oriented approaches.

My professional interests encompass rational medicine use, antimicrobial resistance, and equitable access to quality healthcare. I aspire to bridge pharmaceutical practice with public health research and policy, contributing to sustainable interventions that strengthen health systems and improve outcomes, particularly among underserved populations.
