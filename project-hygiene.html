Handwashing observation checklist
A student is recorded as 'Yes' when all five steps are shown: wet, soap & scrub (palms, backs, fingers, thumbs, nails), scrub ~20 s, rinse, dry. Observed at baseline and after the demonstration.
Survey method
 
DesignPre/post (before-and-after) knowledge survey with week-8 follow-up in all 5 schools; same questionnaire each time.
Participants300 students, grades 4–8, from 2 government and 3 private schools in Chakwal District (see Student Data).
Instrument12-item hygiene knowledge questionnaire (6 topics × 2 items) plus a 5-step handwashing observation checklist.
TimingBaseline on day 1 of each school visit; post-session immediately after the education activities; follow-up in week 8 (results compiled).
EthicsSchool NOCs obtained; parents informed; no names on questionnaires; results reported per school, never per child.
AnalysisMean score, % correct by topic, % correct handwashing, and change between rounds. All summary cells are formulas linked to the Student Data sheet.
Data statusData compiled from attendance registers and completed questionnaires; figures and analysis are based on the available records
 
Statistical testsPaired t-test, 95% confidence interval and effect size (Cohen dz) for the knowledge change; McNemar test for handwashing technique. See the Statistics sheet (all formulas linked to Student Data).
LimitationsNo control group, so change cannot be attributed to the programme alone. The post-test was given straight after the session, so part of the gain may be short-term recall. Knowledge and observed handwashing were measured, not illness or absenteeism. Follow-up handwashing was not observed. Schools were not randomly selected.
InterpretationResults show improved knowledge and observed technique after the sessions in these 5 schools; they should not be generalised beyond this sample.
