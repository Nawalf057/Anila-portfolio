Preserving Our Medicines

Safe antibiotic use in rural communities of Chakwal District, Pakistan.

Community pharmacy project

A pharmacist-led antibiotic stewardship programme educating 200 people in five villages through pharmacy counselling, flyer and poster drives, door-to-door outreach and free medical camps.

5 villagesAround Choa Saidan Shah, Chakwal District
200 respondentsPre/post knowledge survey with 8-week follow-up
March to August 2026DAAD EPOS public health initiative

Key findings

33.5% → 87.5%Knew that antibiotics do not work against colds and flu
67% → 38.5%Bought antibiotics without a prescription (baseline to follow-up)
55.5% → 24%Kept leftover antibiotics at home for later use

Community Antibiotic Stewardship Survey - 200 respondents
 
ProgrammePreserving Our Medicines: Safe Antibiotic Use in Rural Communities (DAAD EPOS Public Health Initiative)
LeadAnila Munir, Clinical & Community Pharmacist
SettingFive villages around Choa Saidan Shah, Chakwal District, Pakistan
DesignPre/post survey. Each respondent answered the same 8-item knowledge questionnaire before the education contact and again after it. Five self-reported practice questions (last 3 months) were asked at baseline and repeated at the 8-week follow-up.
Contact pointsPharmacy counter counselling, door-to-door flyer visits, free medical camps
ScoringKnowledge items: 1 = correct answer, 0 = incorrect. Practice items: 1 = reported the risky practice, 0 = did not.
How to useSheet 'Survey Data' holds one row per respondent (blue text = raw survey entries you can overwrite). Every other sheet is formula-driven and updates automatically when the data change.
