Community Health Triage & Digital Disease Referral

A bilingual mobile app for rural families in Chakwal District, Pakistan.

Digital health project

Chakwal Health Connect is a bilingual (English and Urdu) mobile app that helps families recognise symptoms early, understand the likely illness and reach the right clinic in time. It was piloted door to door in the community.

English and UrduEvery result carries guidance in both languages
Offline decision rulesWorks for families with a basic smartphone
Chakwal DistrictPiloted with families at home

Key findings

WHO IMCIDecision rules built on WHO danger signs and local clinical practice
2 physiciansReviewed the rules at DHQ Hospital Chakwal before the pilot
4 illnesses + danger signsMeasles, chickenpox, dengue and malaria, with emergency referral
