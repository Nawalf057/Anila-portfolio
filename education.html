Education

Academic background at Shifa Tameer-e-Millat University, Islamabad.

A graduate of Shifa Tameer-e-Millat University, Islamabad, trained to deliver safe, evidence-based and patient-centred pharmaceutical care.

My pharmacy education combined a rigorous grounding in pharmaceutical sciences, pharmacology and pharmacotherapeutics with hands-on laboratory training and supervised practice in hospital and community settings. It gave me the clinical reasoning to review prescriptions, anticipate drug-related problems and counsel patients with confidence.

Beyond the curriculum, my time at Shifa shaped my commitment to rational medicine use, antimicrobial stewardship and equitable access to healthcare, the themes that continue to guide my career in clinical and public health pharmacy.
